The Accompaniment Style

Because this song is a romantic, dramatic ballad, your playing needs to use a lot of rubato (flexible tempo).

  • Left Hand (LH): Use wide, arpeggiated bass patterns (Root → 5th → Octave → 10th) to mimic the orchestral strings.

  • Right Hand (RH): Play rich 3 or 4-note block chords to pad beneath the singer's voice. Break into soft arpeggios when the voice holds a long note.

Pro Piano Tips for This Style
